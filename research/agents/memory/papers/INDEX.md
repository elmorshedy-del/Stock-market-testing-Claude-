## Card template
```
# Authors (year). Title. Venue or id
Read: full text (which sections), date. Verified: Crossref or arXiv.
## Claim            (1-2 lines)
## Data             (what, where, when, n)
## Method           (enough to replicate)
## Results          (numbers with errors)
## Caveats          (the authors' and mine)
## What it means for us   (laws, owner's ideas)
## How we would replicate or extend it on our data
## Open questions
```
